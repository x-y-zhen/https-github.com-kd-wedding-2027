David and Katherine cordially invite you to celebrate their union.

This celebration will be held at the Josephine Butler Parks Center, overlooking Meridian Hill and Malcolm X Park. Josephine Butler was an activist and accused Communist who organized the first black women's laundry workers' union, worked to desegregate the schools in Adams Morgan, and advocated for D.C. Statehood. We are excited to welcome our guests to a historic venue, named after a woman whose values we share.
